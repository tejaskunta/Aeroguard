_Arm A threshold = recall-first operator point chosen on val; arm B threshold chosen on val to match arm A's val recall; arm C = YOLO dets at conf>=yolo_conf routed through the MobileNetV3-Large crop classifier with protected cutoff P and classifier threshold t._
_Caveat at detector seed 2: the val-selected matched-recall threshold coincides with the deploy threshold (both 0.21), so arms A and B are the same operating point and the C-B comparison there is against an undegraded YOLO baseline. The two-stage pipeline loses 3.6 points of precision against that baseline, i.e. the gain in T4 comes from seeds whose val-selected YOLO threshold transferred poorly to test, not from seeds where it transferred well. Detector seed 4 fails for a related reason: its val-only selection rule chose a near-inert classifier (protect 0.8, t 0.03), so the classifier vetoes almost nothing and the surviving pool is larger than arm B's. Validation already preferred YOLO-only at that seed (val FP/img 1.448 for C vs 0.956 for A, the only seed of five where this happens), so the regression is a selection-rule artifact rather than a capability signal; see paper_results/analysis/a4_s4_conf_diagnostic.md. This is the central threat to the headline claim and is discussed in §8._
